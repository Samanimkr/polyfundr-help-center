These are the short answers. For the full policy and examples, use [Funded payouts](/funded/payouts).

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What is the profit split?">
  **80% to you, 20% to PolyFundr** on a standard account. Add the **90% profit
  split** upgrade at checkout and you keep 90% instead. See
  [Challenge upgrades](/challenges/upgrades).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When can I request a payout?">
When all of these are true: your **realized eligible profit** (after prior paid payouts) has reached **2% of your starting balance**, your **book is flat** (all positions closed), no other request is pending, and this payout window is **consistent** (no single day produced more than your account's consistency cap of the combined profit of the winning days in the window). For example, on a \$50,000 funded account you become eligible once you have \$1,000 in realized eligible profit.

The 2% is a gate for *requesting*, not a floor your balance can never cross. There is no minimum trading-days requirement, and open positions do not count until closed.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What profit counts toward a payout?">
Losses always count, so nothing is hidden from the calculation. Gains count when the position is closed and it is not a direct same-market YES/NO hedge. Entries outside **0.10 to 0.90** cannot be opened in the first place, so nothing is set aside after the fact. Normal directional trading is fully covered. See [Funded payouts](/funded/payouts) for detail.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I request a payout?">
  Submit the request from the **Payouts** page in your dashboard. Enter a
  destination wallet address, confirm the amount, and submit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How much can I request?">
  Each payout is the lower of two limits: the cap ladder (**5% of your account
  on your first payout, then 10%, up to \$5,000 per payout** gross) and your
  eligible net after prior paid and pending payouts. A **7-day cooldown**
  applies between payouts. Profit above a cap is not lost, it stays in the
  account and remains withdrawable in future windows. Full worked examples on
  [Funded payouts](/funded/payouts).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How fast are payouts processed?">
  Payouts are processed within **24 hours on average**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What currency are payouts in?">
  Payouts are sent in **USDC on the Polygon network**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What kind of wallet address do I need?">
  You must provide a valid **EVM wallet address** that can receive **USDC on
  Polygon**. The destination is required on every request and is not saved as a
  default.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do open positions count toward my payout?">
  No. Only **realized profit from closed positions** counts toward payout
  eligibility.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to the amount I request while it is being processed?">
  Only one payout request can be pending per funded account at a time. While the
  payout is pending, the requested amount is **reserved against your available
  payout balance**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does withdrawing profits affect my drawdown limits?">
  No. Paid payouts are accounted for in the drawdown calculations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are there any fees on payouts?">
  No. There are no payout withdrawal fees.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
