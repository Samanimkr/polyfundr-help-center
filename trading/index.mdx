Trading on PolyFundr means placing simulated YES or NO trades on real Polymarket markets. Nothing executes on-chain: your position is recorded at the current Polymarket price and your live PnL updates as the market moves. Start with [Markets](/trading/markets) to find events, [Placing trades](/trading/placing-trades) for the trade flow, [Fees & execution](/trading/fees) for costs and slippage, and [Positions & PnL](/trading/positions-and-pnl) for tracking.
